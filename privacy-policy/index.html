The application does not verify mobile numbers through OTP, phone calls, passwords, or any other authentication method.

Information We Collect

The application may collect the following information:

  • A mobile number entered by the user before opening the survey form.
  • Information voluntarily entered by the user in the survey form.
  • Technical information required to operate the application, submit survey responses, and communicate with the application server.

The entered mobile number is not verified through OTP or any other verification process. Users are responsible for entering accurate information.

The survey form may contain personal, household, demographic, educational, occupational, economic, location-related, or other information depending on the questions included in the survey. Users should submit only information that they are authorized to provide.

How We Use Information

The information collected through the application may be used to:

  • Record and manage survey submissions.
  • Associate a mobile number with a submitted survey response.
  • Review and organize collected survey information.
  • Generate survey reports and summaries.
  • Allow authorized administrators to manage survey records.
  • Export survey information for authorized administrative use.
  • Maintain and improve the operation of the application.

Mobile Number Verification

The application does not verify the mobile number entered by the user. No OTP, verification phone call, password, or account-verification process is used.

Entering a mobile number does not create a verified user account. The number is used only as a reference associated with the survey submission.

Data Storage and Access

Submitted information may be transmitted to the application's server and stored in its database.

Survey responses may be accessed by authorized administrators responsible for managing the survey, reviewing submissions, preparing reports, and exporting survey data.

Access to collected information is intended to be limited to authorized persons who require it for the operation and administration of the survey.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from an authorized public authority.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect submitted information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or loss.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Survey information may be retained for as long as it is required for survey administration, reporting, record management, legal obligations, or other legitimate purposes connected with the survey.

Correction and Deletion Requests

Users may request correction or deletion of information submitted through the application by contacting us at the email address provided below.

Requests should include enough information to identify the relevant survey submission, such as the mobile number entered with the survey.

Requests may be reviewed subject to administrative, legal, record-keeping, and verification requirements.

The application is intended to be used for authorized survey activities. It is not specifically designed as a children's entertainment or social application.
