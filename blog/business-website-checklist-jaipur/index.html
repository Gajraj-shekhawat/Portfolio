What a Small Business Website Needs: A Checklist for 2026

Most customers find you on a phone, through Google, and want to message you on WhatsApp. Build for that.

Most small business owners I meet in Jaipur don't need a complicated website. They need one that loads fast on a phone, shows up on Google and makes it easy for customers to call or message. Here is the checklist I use.

The essentials

  • Fast on mobile. Most visitors are on phones, often on slow connections. Aim for the page to load in about two seconds.
  • Clear first screen. What you do, where you are, and one button to call or WhatsApp you.
  • WhatsApp and click-to-call buttons on every page.
  • Your address, hours and a map, matching your Google Business Profile exactly.
  • Real photos of your shop, products or work, not stock images.
  • An enquiry form that sends leads straight to your email or WhatsApp.

Getting found on Google

  • A verified Google Business Profile with the same name, address and phone as the website.
  • A page for each main service, with the city in the title (for example "Interior designer in Jaipur").
  • Proper page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, and the site added to Google Search Console.
  • Ask happy customers for Google reviews. They matter as much as the website itself.

Nice to have

  • Online payments (Razorpay or Cashfree) if customers book or buy online.
  • A simple catalogue or menu you can update yourself.
  • Hindi and English if your customers use both.

What to avoid

  • Heavy sliders and animations that slow the site down on phones.
  • A site you can't update without calling the developer every time.
  • Paying yearly for a website builder when a fast custom site would cost less over time.
