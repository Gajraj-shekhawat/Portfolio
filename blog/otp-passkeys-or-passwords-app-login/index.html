OTP, Passkeys or Passwords: Which Login Should Your App Use?

For most Indian customer apps, phone OTP is the right start. Passkeys are the future. Passwords alone are the weakest choice.

Login is the first thing every user sees, and the first thing attackers try. Here is how the three common options compare, and what I recommend for different apps.

Phone number + OTP

  • Best for: customer apps in India, shop owners, anyone who doesn't want to remember a password.
  • Good: familiar to everyone; no password to forget or leak.
  • Watch out: SMS costs money per message and delivery can be patchy. Add a WhatsApp fallback, limit attempts, and expire codes quickly.

Passkeys

  • Best for: returning users on modern phones and laptops; dashboards and admin panels.
  • Good: sign in with fingerprint or face; cannot be phished or reused on another site.
  • Watch out: users switching devices need a backup method, so pair passkeys with OTP or email.

Email + password

  • Best for: internal tools, when nothing else is possible.
  • Watch out: people reuse passwords. If you must use them, store them with a strong hashing algorithm, add rate limits, and offer two-factor authentication.

My recommendation

  • Customer app in India: phone OTP (SMS + WhatsApp fallback), with passkeys added for returning users.
  • Staff and admin panels: passkeys or OTP plus a second factor, and role-based access.
  • Whatever you choose, run the login on your own backend so you control limits, logs and costs.
