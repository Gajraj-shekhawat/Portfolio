How to Build an E-commerce Website: A Planning Checklist

Before you build an online store, answer these questions. This checklist turns a vague idea into a clear plan you can hand to any developer.

An online store is more than a website with a cart. Most of the work is in the decisions around it. Go through this checklist before you build, whether you do it yourself or hire someone.

1. Decide what kind of store

  • B2C (selling to consumers), B2B (shops and distributors reordering in bulk), or both?
  • Website only, or also a mobile app?
  • How many products, and do they have variants (size, colour, pack)?

2. Shopify or custom-built?

  • Shopify / WooCommerce: quick to start for standard retail with a monthly fee and apps for extras.
  • Custom: better when you have special pricing rules, B2B ordering, credit limits, your own delivery team, or want to avoid per-order fees as you grow.

3. Feature checklist

  • Product catalogue with search, filters and good photos.
  • Cart and checkout in as few steps as possible; guest checkout or phone OTP sign-in.
  • Payments: UPI, cards, net banking (Razorpay vs Cashfree), and cash on delivery if you offer it.
  • Shipping: delivery zones, charges, and a courier integration or your own delivery app.
  • Order updates by SMS, email and WhatsApp.
  • GST invoices generated automatically (what to get right).
  • Admin panel: products, stock, orders, refunds, customers and reports.
  • Coupons and offers, if you'll use them.

4. Getting found

  • SEO-friendly product and category pages with real descriptions.
  • Fast loading on mobile (Core Web Vitals).
  • Google Merchant Center for free product listings, and a Google Business Profile if you have a shop.

5. Before launch

  • Test a real order end to end, including a refund.
  • Add privacy, refund, shipping and terms pages (payment gateways require them).
  • Set up backups, monitoring and analytics.
