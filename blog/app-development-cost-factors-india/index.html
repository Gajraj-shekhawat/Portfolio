What Decides the Cost of Building an App in India

Two apps that look similar can differ in cost several times over. These are the things that make the difference, and how to keep your first version lean.

"How much will my app cost?" is the first question almost every client asks. The honest answer is that it depends on a handful of things. Knowing them helps you plan a budget and avoid paying for features you don't need yet.

The biggest cost drivers

  1. Number of user types. A customer app alone is one thing. A customer app, a staff app and an admin panel is three products sharing one backend.
  2. Platforms. Android only, Android + iOS, and web. React Native lets one codebase cover Android and iOS, which saves a lot.
  3. Integrations. Payments (Razorpay, Cashfree), SMS/WhatsApp OTP, maps, shipping, GST invoicing, accounting software. Each one adds work and testing.
  4. Business rules. Pricing schemes, discounts, stock, delivery slots. Rules are where most of the real work hides.
  5. Design. A clean standard design is quick; a fully custom design with animations takes longer.
  6. Quality. Automated tests, security checks and monitoring cost a little more up front and save much more later.
  7. After launch. Hosting, app-store updates, fixes and new features. Plan for ongoing maintenance from day one.

How to keep version 1 lean

  • Write down the one job the app must do well, and build only that first.
  • Start with Android if most of your customers use it, and add iOS when the app proves itself.
  • Use a simple admin panel instead of building every report on day one.
  • Ask for a fixed quote per milestone, with a demo on a live link at each step.
