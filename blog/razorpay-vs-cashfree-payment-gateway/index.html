Razorpay vs Cashfree: Choosing a Payment Gateway in India

I've integrated both in production. Either is a good choice; the integration done right matters more than the brand.

I've used Razorpay for admissions and fee payments at a coaching institute and Cashfree for orders at a wholesale business. Both work well. Here is how I'd choose, and the integration mistakes to avoid with either.

What both give you

  • UPI, cards, net banking and wallets through one integration.
  • Hosted checkout pages and SDKs for websites and Android/iOS apps.
  • Payment links you can send on WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Dashboards for refunds, settlements and reports.

How I choose

  • Compare current pricing for your business type directly with each provider; rates and offers change and are often negotiable at volume.
  • Check settlement time and whether you need faster settlements.
  • Check payouts if you need to pay vendors, staff or refunds in bulk.
  • Check the onboarding for your business type; some categories need extra documents.
  • Look at the docs and SDKs for your stack. Both have solid Node.js support.

Integration rules I always follow

  1. Create orders on the server, with the amount calculated on the server, never taken from the browser or app.
  2. Verify every payment on the server: check the signature and fetch the payment status from the gateway before marking an order paid.
  3. Use webhooks as well, so payments that complete after the user closes the app still get recorded.
  4. Make it idempotent: the same webhook may arrive twice; it must never mark an order paid twice or send two invoices.
  5. Log everything and reconcile daily against the gateway's settlement report.
