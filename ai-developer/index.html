04 FAQ

Common questions

Can AI be added to an existing product?

Yes. Most AI work is added to existing systems: a question-answering agent over your database, document reading, or summaries of calls and tickets.

Can the AI run without sending data to OpenAI or Google?

Yes. He has deployed self-hosted models (Qwen on vLLM, Ollama) and CPU-only computer vision so sensitive data stays on your servers.

Which AI projects has Gajraj built?

Yugam Ask (text-to-SQL agent over 1.7M schools), Yugam AI (lesson planner and Indian-language call summaries), face-recognition attendance, and AI-read OMR sheets for StreamFit.
