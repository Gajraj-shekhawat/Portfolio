Case study · Food & retail

Data and apps for a 500-store coffee chain

Pickup Coffee is one of the fastest-growing coffee chains in the Philippines and is expanding into Mexico. Working at Shuru Technologies, I first built its data platform and then worked on the backends of its three apps.

Role
Product Engineer (Backend), Shuru
Scale
500+ stores, thousands of daily orders
Impact
30% lower API latency, 40% less DB load
Stack
Node.js, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Airflow, BigQuery

01 The challenge

Fast growth meant data scattered across systems and APIs that slowed down under load. Marketing needed reliable numbers, and the apps needed to stay fast as order volume grew.

02 What I built

  • Apache Airflow pipelines moving data from Google Cloud Storage into BigQuery, and Metabase dashboards for the marketing team.
  • Backend work for the customer ordering app, the barista tablet app used in stores, and the back office for menus and pricing.
  • Node.js (Express) microservices for orders, wallet, vouchers, promotions and stamps on MySQL and PostgreSQL, tuned with query optimisation and Redis caching.
  • Payment integrations for the Philippines and Mexico, including Maya, GCash and debit/credit cards.
  • OpenTelemetry + Jaeger tracing across 5+ services so issues are found and fixed faster.
  • The referral program end to end: RFC, flows, campaign APIs, TalonOne integration and queues for rewards.

03 Results

  • 30% lower API latency
  • 40% less database load and 60% fewer slow queries
  • Debugging time roughly halved with distributed tracing
  • One source of truth for marketing in BigQuery and Metabase
